How to find them

Searching my name only shows the free ones. Here's where the rest live.

Step 1Get access

Join, and put your TradingView username on your Whop profile. Access is added to your TradingView account, usually the same day.

Step 2Open Indicators on a chart

On any TradingView chart, click Indicators at the top.

Step 3Click Invite-only scripts

It's a tab on the left side of the Indicators window. All 8 RTP indicators you have access to are listed there.

OrUse the links below

Tap any indicator here, then hit the star to add it to your favorites or Add to chart.

TradingView's search never shows invite-only scripts, so typing BNice_FNP only brings up the free ones. That's TradingView, not your access.
